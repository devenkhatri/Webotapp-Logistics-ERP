# MASTER DEVELOPMENT PROMPT
# Courier & Logistics Management Software – Complete ERP

## 1. PROJECT OVERVIEW

Act as a senior software architect, Laravel developer, database architect, UI/UX designer, QA engineer, and logistics domain expert.

Build a complete, production-ready, enterprise-grade **Courier & Logistics Management System** using Laravel, PHP, and MySQL.

The software must support the complete operational lifecycle of courier and logistics businesses, including:

- Local courier services
- Domestic courier services
- International courier services
- Express and standard delivery
- Same-day and next-day delivery
- E-commerce shipping
- B2B and B2C logistics
- Third-party logistics (3PL)
- Full truckload (FTL) and less-than-truckload (LTL) logistics
- Franchise-based courier networks
- Warehouse and distribution operations
- Reverse logistics and return-to-origin (RTO)
- Cash on Delivery (COD)
- Corporate and bulk shipping accounts

This must be a functional business application, not just a dashboard or UI prototype.

Every module must have working CRUD operations, validation, database relationships, business logic, role-based access, filters, reports, and operational workflows.

Build the software with a modular architecture so that individual modules can be maintained, extended, and upgraded independently.

The system must support multiple companies, branches, hubs, warehouses, franchise locations, employees, delivery agents, and customers.

### Weight calculation

Actual weight = measured package weight.

Volumetric weight = (Length × Width × Height) / configured volumetric divisor.

Chargeable weight = greater of actual weight and volumetric weight, subject to the company's rounding and minimum-weight rules.

The volumetric divisor and rounding rules must be configurable by service and carrier.

# 36. CRITICAL BUSINESS RULES

Implement the following rules carefully.

1. Every shipment must have a unique AWB number within the configured uniqueness scope.
2. A shipment must have valid sender and receiver information.
3. A shipment cannot be booked without valid package details.
4. Chargeable weight must follow the configured weight rules.
5. Pricing must be calculated using the applicable rate card at booking time.
6. Historical shipment charges must not change when future rate cards are updated.
7. A shipment must not be assigned to an unsupported route or unserviceable destination.
8. Shipment status transitions must follow configured workflow rules.
9. A shipment cannot be marked delivered without the required delivery proof.
10. COD collection cannot exceed the configured collectible amount without authorized adjustment.
11. COD settlement must not be duplicated.
12. A shipment cannot appear in two active dispatch manifests simultaneously unless an explicit split-shipment workflow allows it.
13. Shipment scans must be idempotent where duplicate scans would otherwise cause operational errors.
14. Failed delivery must require a failure reason.
15. RTO must follow an approved return workflow.
16. Financial transactions must create corresponding ledger entries.
17. Posted invoices and settlements must be corrected through reversal or adjustment workflows, not silent edits.
18. Only authorized users can cancel, delete, hold, or change high-value shipments.
19. Public tracking must never expose private customer or financial information.
20. Every important operational and financial change must be recorded in the audit log.
